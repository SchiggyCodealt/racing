# Audio credits

Every sound in Track Rush is made by the project itself. No third-party
recordings, samples, loops or music are used, so there is nothing to attribute
and no outside licence applies to these files.

| What | Where | How it is made |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engines (all six cars) | none on disk | synthesized live while racing by `audio/engine_voice.gd` (firing pulses, exhaust pipe, body resonances, intake, turbo, crackle) |
| Effects (32 files) | `assets/audio/sfx/*.wav` | generated by `tools/gen_sounds.py` (numpy noise, filters, modal and FM synthesis) |
| Music (4 tracks) | `assets/audio/music/*.ogg` | composed and rendered by `tools/gen_sounds.py` (wavetable synths, drum synthesis, delay and reverb), encoded with ffmpeg libvorbis |

To rebuild them: `python3 tools/gen_sounds.py` (needs numpy and ffmpeg).
`--only sfx` or `--only music` and `--names a,b` limit what is made. The
generator is seeded, so the same script gives the same files.

Music tracks:

- `menu.ogg`: "Pit Lane", laid-back house, 108 BPM, 16 bars (menus)
- `overdrive.ogg`: "Overdrive", bright electro house, 128 BPM, 32 bars (meadow, seaside and highland tracks)
- `neon.ogg`: "Neon Rush", driving trance, 138 BPM, 32 bars (night, dusk and arctic tracks)
- `dust.ogg`: "Dust Devil", rally breakbeat, 164 BPM, 32 bars (desert, canyon, alpine and autumn tracks)
